## Examples of changed judgment
1. I kept secondary-warehouse stock separate from main-warehouse availability because XYZ ships orders from the main warehouse. This makes transfer requirements visible instead of treating all stock as immediately available.

2. I kept the prototype focused on operational visibility rather than adding unnecessary integrations. The assignment provides no real store or courier systems, so I used sample data and focused on priority/SLA visibility, inventory checks, exceptions, and pickup monitoring.
